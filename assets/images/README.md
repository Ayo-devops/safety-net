# Illustrative photography

Created with the built-in image generation tool. These fictional scenes do not depict actual Safetynet staff or beneficiaries. Optimized WebP files and 640px variants are stored in this folder. The founder portrait is intentionally pending.

## community.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one landscape editorial photograph for the hero of a Nigerian community support website, Safetynet. Three fictional Black Nigerian adults, two women and one man, sitting together at a wooden table in a bright welcoming community courtyard, enjoying a relaxed supportive conversation. Natural unposed smiles, attentive eye contact between them, no looking at camera. Medium wide waist-up composition, faces clearly visible and grouped in the central area so image can crop to portrait. Soft afternoon daylight, cream plaster walls, leafy greenery, warm earth tones, muted forest green clothing accents. Authentic natural skin texture, tasteful documentary photography, realistic hands. Warm, dignified, hopeful, understated. No text, logos, watermarks, dramatic distress, fundraising signs or staged handshakes. This is illustrative imagery, not actual beneficiaries. Output landscape 1536x1024.

## volunteers.webp

Three fictional Black Nigerian adult volunteers, women and a man, casually working together packing fresh vegetables and pantry staples into plain paper bags at a wooden table in an airy cream-walled community room with leafy plants. Warm spontaneous smiles and concentration, natural interaction, dignified equal participants. Medium wide view, faces grouped in upper middle, table below, no one looking at camera. Muted forest green, cream, terracotta clothing, warm soft daylight. Authentic skin and hands, realistic candid photography. No words, brands, logos, watermarks, poverty imagery or signs. This is an illustrative scene, not actual staff or beneficiaries.

Candid collaborative learning, equal dignified participants, natural skin and hands. Airy cream plaster room, small leafy plant, warm soft window light. Muted sage and ochre casual clothing. Medium wide shot showing faces and laptop with plain unbranded back towards camera. Warm believable understated documentary photography. No text, logos, watermarks or staged thumbs up. Illustrative people, not actual programme participants.
