Before you talk

A Few Guidelines for Your Conversation

  • Call yourself “Service User.” You do not need to introduce yourself by your real name when asking for emotional support.
  • Focus on what you are feeling. You can talk about stress, anxiety, feeling neglected or intimidated, loneliness, or other emotional difficulties. Share at your own pace; you can skip a question or end the conversation.
  • Leave out identifying information. Do not share your name, exact location, home address, school or workplace, helping ID, identity documents, passwords, verification codes, or financial details. Avoid identifying other people in your story too.
  • Keep the conversation respectful. Threats, harassment, and abusive messages are not acceptable. You can stop the chat if you feel uncomfortable.
  • Ask for help with concerns. If a contact pressures you for personal details or makes you uncomfortable, end the conversation and report the concern to safetynetorgng@gmail.com. A report sent by email reveals your email address.

A label alone does not make a chat anonymous. WhatsApp reveals your phone number and may show profile information. The planned website chat is not yet available, and we cannot promise complete anonymity or absolute confidentiality.

You could begin with: “Hello, I’m a Service User. I’ve been feeling stressed and neglected, and I’d like someone to listen.”

These privacy guidelines concern support conversations. If you need emergency help, give the emergency responder the information they need to assist you.
