About Safetynet

Our Story

Safetynet was founded on a simple conviction: support shouldn't be conditional, and protection shouldn't be a privilege.

Our Mission

To provide unconditional support, protection, and empowerment to individuals and families navigating hardship, while partnering with organisations that want to build lasting social impact.

Our Values

What guides every decision we make.

Dignity First

No one who comes to us is made to feel small for asking.

Confidentiality

What you share with us stays between us.

Practical Help

We focus on what actually moves people forward: food, funds, tools, and someone to talk to.

Partnership Over Charity

With organisations, we build alongside, not just for.

Morohunranti Oluwatosin Adebowale

Founder, Safetynet

Morohunranti founded Safetynet out of hands-on social work experience — seeing firsthand how a small amount of timely support (a meal, a listening ear, a micro-loan) can be the difference between someone falling further behind or getting back on their feet. Safetynet is the structure built around that belief: care that doesn't ask for anything in return, and consulting that helps larger organisations do the same at scale.
